More than a number of grafts.

Design

A hairline should belong to the person.

Natural-looking design considers proportion, direction, density, age and the wider long-term plan—not a single fashionable shape.

Individual characteristics

Texture changes the planning conversation.

Curl pattern and follicle characteristics can influence assessment and technique. Claims of suitability require a clinician-led evaluation.

Browse all FAQs ↗
Why does large-session planning need care?

Donor supply, treatment area, health and long-term goals all matter. A graft number alone cannot establish suitability; only an individual clinical assessment can do that.

What should be considered for Afro-textured hair?

Hair and follicle characteristics vary between people. A clinician should explain their relevant experience, how they assess your individual characteristics, and the options, limitations and risks.

How long does recovery take?

Recovery differs by person and procedure. Ask the treating clinician for written aftercare, expected time away from work and clear guidance on when to seek help.

How can I verify a clinician's registration?

Use the official GMC register to check the clinician's current registration and details before relying on a registration claim.
